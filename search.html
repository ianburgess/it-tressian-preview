  • Our Group

    We operate two regulated water companies and also offer a range of non regulated services and products to essential services operators and markets.

    Our Businesses
    • South Staffs Water Regulated clean water supplier to customers in West Midlands, South Staffordshire, South Derbyshire, North Warwickshire and North Worcestershire.
    • Cambridge Water Regulated clean water supplier to customers in Cambridge, from Ramsey in the north, Gamlingay in the west, Balsham in the east and Melbourn in the south.
    • Advanced Engineering Solutions (external site)Water and gas pipeline and associated asset engineering specialist
    • G Stow (external site)Borehole drilling and maintenance experts
    • Hydrosave (external site)Water sector leak detection and technical services specialist
    • Integrated Water Services (M&E) (external site)Offers services to mitigate and manage risk from mechanical and electrical failures
    • OnSite Maintaining water and wastewater essential infrastructure and assets
    • Omega Red (external site)Earthing, lightning protection, surge protection, height safety and EV charging services
    • Echo Managed Services (external site)Water sector customer service and billing software specialist
    • Aqua Direct (external site)UK’s Premier Supplier of Finest Tanker Spring Water
    • Office Watercoolers (external site)Provides and services high quality bottled water and mains fed dispensers
    • Infrastructure Gateway Ltd (IGL) (external site)The UK’s largest independent multi utility infrastructure provider
